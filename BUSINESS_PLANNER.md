# Heritage Joiners — AI Business Operating System (v1)
Date: 25 Apr 2026

## Mission
Dominate Yorkshire as the leading timber repair specialist for listed and period buildings.

## Operating Model
Rob = delivery + craft + client decisions
Hermes AI Team = admin, strategy, research, website content QA, SEO execution support, reporting cadence

---

## 1) Command KPIs (tracked weekly)

### Lead & conversion
- New enquiries (count)
- Qualified enquiries (count)
- Quote requests (count)
- Quote-to-job win rate (%)
- Avg job value (£)

### Website & search
- Sessions (total)
- Organic sessions
- Top landing pages
- WhatsApp click count
- Phone click count
- Contact form submissions
- Top search queries + average position

### Proof & authority
- New before/during/after sets published
- New case studies published
- New reviews/testimonials captured

---

## 2) Daily cadence (Mon–Fri)

1. Check open leads and response status
2. Check website conversion paths (WhatsApp/phone/form)
3. Review yesterday’s performance notes
4. Log blockers and immediate fixes
5. Set top 3 actions for the day

Deliverable: short Daily Ops Note (what changed, what matters, next actions)

---

## 3) Weekly cadence (every Sunday)

1. KPI review (lead, conversion, SEO)
2. Page performance review (what pages drive enquiries)
3. Keyword movement review (priority terms)
4. Competitor movement review (Yorkshire repair-first firms)
5. Priority decision list for next 7 days

Deliverable: Weekly Growth Brief with:
- Wins
- Problems
- Root causes
- 7-day action plan
- Owner tags (Rob vs Hermes)

---

## 4) Monthly cadence

1. Service-page message tightening
2. Case-study publishing sprint
3. Offer/pricing review
4. Territory expansion review (Yorkshire coverage map)

Deliverable: Monthly Strategy Update with next-month execution priorities.

---

## 5) Workstreams owned by Hermes

### A) Admin & systems
- Keep strategy docs coherent
- Maintain checklist discipline
- Keep action tracker current

### B) Website growth support
- Copy blocks and CTA optimization suggestions
- Service page and FAQ refinements
- Internal linking recommendations
- SEO meta/title/H1 QA

### C) Research & monitoring
- Competitor tracking (Yorkshire)
- Keyword priority monitoring support
- Offer positioning checks

### D) Reporting
- Daily note structure
- Weekly growth brief
- Monthly strategy summary

---

## 6) Non-negotiables

- Repair-first, not repair-at-any-cost
- Whole-Yorkshire domination goal
- Evidence-first marketing (real photos, real projects)
- No fluff pages; only pages that rank and convert

---

## 7) Priority queue template (rolling)

1. Revenue now (lead/conversion blockers)
2. Authority now (case studies/proof)
3. SEO compounding (core pages + technical hygiene)
4. Expansion (new Yorkshire area coverage)

---

## 8) Execution principle

Small weekly gains, no drift:
- improve one conversion element,
- improve one ranking element,
- publish one authority element.

Repeat every week.
