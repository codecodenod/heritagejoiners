# Competition Ideas (Case Studies)

Last updated: 2026-05-03

## 1) YourSashWindows.com
Source: http://yoursashwindows.com/

### What they’re doing well
- **Service silo structure is very clear**: separate pages for restoration, repair, replacement, draught-proofing, double glazing, heritage, and sliding sash sub-types.
- **Strong conversion pathway** repeated site-wide: “Request a Survey” appears as the main CTA.
- **Trust-positioning language** is consistent: repeated mention of years of experience, volume of windows restored, and UK-wide capability.
- **Covers conservation/heritage intent** with dedicated heritage and regulation-focused pages.
- **Broad offer scope** includes sash windows, casement windows, and doors/doorsets, giving cross-sell potential.

### Weaknesses / gaps you can exploit
- Messaging appears **general UK-wide**, not deeply localized by area/city intent.
- “Repair vs replacement” language is present, but there is less visible emphasis on a strict, evidence-led **preservation-first diagnostic framework**.
- Brand differentiation is more “full-service window company” than “listed/period timber repair specialist authority.”
- CTA is mostly one type (“Request a Survey”) — limited educational lead magnets (guides, checklists, planning help).

### Heritage Joiners opportunities (Yorkshire domination strategy)
1. **Own the preservation-first position**
   - Lead with: on-site repair first where feasible, minimal disturbance, retain original fabric.
   - Explicitly state your decision rule: repair-first, but not repair-at-any-cost when structural retention is too low.

2. **Build Yorkshire location clusters**
   - Dedicated pages for York, Leeds, Harrogate, Sheffield, Wakefield, Huddersfield, Ripon, Scarborough, etc.
   - Add conservation-area and listed-building nuances per location.

3. **Publish a transparent repair viability framework**
   - Simple staged assessment model: retain / selective splice / partial replacement / full replacement.
   - Explain trigger points with practical examples and photos.

4. **Create stronger proof assets than competitors**
   - Before/after case studies with:
     - timber retained (%)
     - defects found
     - intervention method
     - disturbance avoided
     - expected service life outcome

5. **Expand conversion routes**
   - Keep “Request Survey,” but add:
     - “Listed Building Timber Repair Starter Guide”
     - “Conservation-friendly window repair checklist”
     - “Photo assessment upload” lead form

6. **Differentiate by specialist language**
   - Competitor uses broad window-company framing.
   - You should frame as **period/listed timber pathology + conservation-conscious joinery intervention specialist**.
