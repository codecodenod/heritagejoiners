# Competition Ideas (Case Studies)

Last updated: 2026-05-03

## Executive summary

Heritage Joiners' real competitive set is not "sash window companies" — it is a small group of repair-first, personal-brand specialists in North Yorkshire (Harrogate, York), one Manchester-based repair-first ltd company covering Yorkshire online, and a national multi-location operator (Yoursashwindows) actively targeting Leeds and York. Ventrolla sits above all of them as the premium replacement anchor.

The strategic picture is:

- The WF postcode area (Pontefract WF8, Castleford WF10, Featherstone, Wakefield WF1–4) has no specialist repair-first competitor with a real online presence. This is Heritage Joiners' uncontested home territory.
- Harrogate and York are crowded with strong personal-brand operators (NY Sash Windows, Sash Window Restoration, The Sash Man, Sashwindows-york).
- Leeds is contested by Yoursashwindows.com and The Leeds Sash Windows Co, both replacement-capable rather than repair-only.
- Brand confusion risk is real: Heritage Joinery Solutions Ltd (Manchester), Heritage Joinery Whitby, and the fact that NY Sash Windows is also founded by a "Rob" with 20+ years' experience.

Competitive strategy follows from that picture: own WF postcodes outright, build credentialling that mirrors NY Sash (Wakefield Council conservation approval), publish a preservation-first diagnostic framework no competitor has, and use Ventrolla replacement pricing as a conversion anchor on every service page.

## Competitor case studies

### 1) YourSashWindows.com

Source: <https://yoursashwindows.com/>
Phone: 0333 600 0196
Coverage: National — Leeds, York, Manchester, Harrogate, Chester, Liverpool, North West
Experience claim: 15+ years restoration

#### What they're doing well

- Service silo structure is very clear: separate pages for restoration, repair, replacement, draught-proofing, double glazing, heritage, and sliding sash sub-types.
- Strong conversion pathway repeated site-wide: "Request a Survey" appears as the main CTA.
- Trust-positioning language is consistent: repeated mention of years of experience, volume of windows restored, and UK-wide capability.
- Covers conservation/heritage intent with dedicated heritage and regulation-focused pages.
- Multi-location SEO structure: dedicated /leeds/ and /york/ pages already live and active — they are already inside Heritage Joiners' secondary territory.
- Broad offer scope includes sash windows, casement windows, and doors/doorsets, giving cross-sell potential.
- Linked group operation with DHW Joinery (York) gives them depth on listed building replacement.

#### Weaknesses / gaps you can exploit

- Messaging is general UK-wide, not deeply localized by area/city intent — the location pages exist but the content underneath is thin and templated.
- "Repair vs replacement" language is present, but there is less visible emphasis on a strict, evidence-led preservation-first diagnostic framework.
- Brand differentiation is more "full-service window company" than "listed/period timber repair specialist authority."
- CTA is mostly one type ("Request a Survey") — limited educational lead magnets.
- 0333 number signals national call centre, not a local craftsman — Heritage Joiners' WF or 07 mobile feels more personal in the WF/Leeds/York triangle.
- No personal founder story — the brand is faceless. Rob's 35+ years and Counting House case study win on every personal-trust dimension.
- Replacement is part of the offer, so anyone wanting a guarantee that their original timber will be preserved gets a softer answer here.

#### Heritage Joiners opportunities

1. Own the preservation-first position
- Lead with: on-site repair first where feasible, minimal disturbance, retain original fabric.
   - State your decision rule explicitly: repair-first, but not repair-at-any-cost when structural retention is too low.
2. Build a tighter Yorkshire location structure than they have
   - Phase 1 priority: Pontefract, Wakefield, Castleford, Featherstone (uncontested).
   - Phase 2: Leeds, York (where Yoursashwindows already ranks — fight on depth, not breadth).
   - Each page must carry conservation-area and listed-building nuances specific to that locality, not template copy.
3. Publish a transparent repair viability framework
   - Staged assessment model: retain / selective splice / partial replacement / full replacement.
   - Trigger points with practical examples and photos from real Heritage Joiners jobs.
   - This is the single hardest page for Yoursashwindows to copy — it requires the judgement of a 35+ year tradesman, not a marketing team.
4. Create stronger proof assets
   - Before/after case studies with timber retained (%), defects found, intervention method, disturbance avoided, expected service life outcome.
   - Counting House Pontefract as primary anchor, with 3–4 additional documented jobs in year one.
5. Differentiate by specialist language
   - Yoursashwindows uses broad window-company framing.
   - Heritage Joiners frames as period/listed timber pathology + conservation-conscious joinery intervention specialist.
6. Hold the line on WhatsApp photo-first CTA
   - Don't dilute by chasing their "Request a Survey" pattern. WhatsApp triage filters out low-value leads — a deliberate operations choice, not a conversion gap.

#### Evidence captured

- Homepage title: "Sash Window Restoration & Double Glazing | Casement Window Repairs"
- Dedicated pages found: /sash-windows/, /sash-windows/sash-window-restoration/, /sash-windows/sash-window-double-glazing/, /sash-windows/sash-window-replacement/, /sash-windows/sash-window-draught-proofing/, /sash-windows/sash-window-repair/, /sash-windows/heritage-sash-windows/, /sash-windows/sliding-sash-windows/, /leeds/, /york/
- Repeated CTA: "Request a Survey"

### 2) North Yorkshire Sash Windows

Source: <https://www.nysashwindows.co.uk/>
Coverage: Harrogate, Ripon, Thirsk, Boroughbridge, Wetherby, Easingwold, most of North Yorkshire
Founder: Rob — 20+ years, started 2007
Threat level: HIGH — most directly comparable competitor

#### What they're doing well

- Strict repair-only positioning: "We specialise in the repair, refurbishment and draught proofing of traditional sash windows. We don't replace them, we don't paint them. We restore them, with painstaking care." — almost verbatim Heritage Joiners' core philosophy.
- Genuine credentialling that's hard to match: on the contractors list for North York Moors National Park and Harrogate Borough Council Conservation Department.
- Architect endorsement on homepage from Peter Pearson, Architectural Historian — exactly the proof asset Heritage Joiners is trying to build with Guy Lister.
- Family business framing — small, personal, geographically rooted (Harrogate/Ripon).
- Personal brand around the founder — Rob, 20+ years, since 2007. Same template Heritage Joiners is using.

#### Weaknesses / gaps you can exploit

- Limited content depth — fewer service silos and less educational content than larger competitors.
- Fewer visible conversion layers — no obvious multi-step lead journey like guides, checklists, or case-study funnels.
- Less visible technical framework explaining when repair is viable vs when replacement becomes necessary.
- Less obvious proof architecture — no visible before/after technical evidence, retention metrics, or intervention detail beyond the architect quote.
- Geographic ceiling — Harrogate/Ripon/Thirsk-focused. Wetherby is the southernmost coverage (LS23 in North Leeds). They do not cover WF postcodes.
- No visible reviews on homepage — relies on the Pearson quote alone.
- Thin website — every additional page Heritage Joiners publishes widens the SEO gap.

#### Heritage Joiners opportunities

1. Out-educate with technical authority
   - Publish timber defect diagnostics, repair sequence logic, and viability thresholds — the framework NY Sash hasn't built.
2. Out-proof with measurable case studies
   - Include timber retained %, defects mapped, method used, expected lifespan gain. Counting House plus 3–4 documented year-one jobs gives a deeper proof stack than a single architect quote.
3. Mirror their credentialling in West Yorkshire
   - Pursue Wakefield Council conservation approval as a direct equivalent to NY Sash's Harrogate Borough Council listing.
   - Approach the relevant heritage and conservation bodies with WY remit to build the equivalent contractor-list status.
4. Out-convert with tiered CTAs
   - WhatsApp photo intake (primary), site visit booking, plus a downloadable listed-building repair guide and photo assessment upload as supporting routes.
5. Out-rank in West Yorkshire
   - NY Sash doesn't compete in WF postcodes at all. Heritage Joiners' Pontefract, Castleford, Featherstone, Wakefield pages can dominate without overlap.
6. Manage the brand-confusion risk
   - Both businesses have a "Rob" founder with 20+ years' experience and a repair-first stance. In the Wetherby/North Leeds overlap zone, monitor for naming confusion and lean harder on "Heritage Joiners" + Pontefract/WF identifiers in metadata, GBP, and ad copy.

#### Evidence captured

- Homepage title: "North Yorkshire Sash Windows - Expert Restoration and Draught Proofing Services"
- Core headings: Restoration, Draught Proofing, About, Contact
- Primary CTA: "Contact Us"
- Brand framing: family business serving Harrogate/surrounding area
- Endorsement: quote from Peter Pearson, Architectural Historian
- Credentials: NYM National Park contractors list, Harrogate Borough Council Conservation Department

### 3) Sash Window Restoration (Stephen Westerman)

Source: <https://www.sashwindowsrestoration.co.uk/>
Phone: 07813 407 917
Coverage: Harrogate, Knaresborough, across North Yorkshire
Experience: 20+ years
Threat level: HIGH — strongest personal-brand parallel after NY Sash

#### What they're doing well

- Personal craftsman brand built around Stephen Westerman — same template Heritage Joiners is using around Rob.
- 20+ years of experience as the headline trust signal.
- Customer testimonial visible on homepage — a small detail NY Sash hasn't done.
- Dedicated previous-work page at /previous-work/ — proof architecture exists.
- Tight service set that matches Heritage Joiners almost exactly: restoration, draught proofing, glazing replacement, painting.
- Mobile-first contact route — 07 number signals direct access to the craftsman.

#### Weaknesses / gaps you can exploit

- Geographic ceiling — Harrogate and Knaresborough centred, no WF coverage.
- No visible credentials — no conservation department approvals, no architect quotes, no industry membership visible.
- Painting is in the service mix — Heritage Joiners' explicit decision to avoid painting-led positioning is a sharper niche.
- Limited educational depth — no preservation-first framework or repair-vs-replace decision content visible.
- No volume proof — no project counts, no measurable retention metrics, no listed-building portfolio breakdown.
- Single founder dependency — Heritage Joiners' Counting House plus future case studies build a more institutional proof stack.

#### Heritage Joiners opportunities
1. Out-credential them
   - Conservation department listings, listed-building indemnity wording, professional indemnity cover, CSCS as required — make the credentialling visible on the homepage.
2. Out-narrative them with the listed/period story
   - Stephen frames as "sash restoration specialist." Heritage Joiners frames wider: sash, doors, frames, rot and splice repairs, and integrated listed-property work. The wider repair scope plus apprenticeship-trained background reads as more authoritative on full-property heritage work.
3. Out-prove them with measurable case studies
   - Their previous-work page shows photos. Heritage Joiners' Counting House case study with timber retained %, defects mapped, and intervention method goes deeper.
4. Avoid head-to-head in Harrogate
   - Don't fight Westerman in his core area. Compete in WF postcodes, then Leeds and York where his presence is weaker.

#### Evidence captured

- Homepage shows customer testimonial
- Dedicated /previous-work/ page
- 20+ years' experience as primary trust signal
- Service set: restoration, draught proofing, glazing replacement, painting
- Personal brand built around founder Stephen Westerman

### 4) Heritage Joinery Solutions Ltd

Source: <https://www.heritagejoinerysolutionsltd.com/>
Phone: 0161 486 9988
Coverage: Manchester base — covers Yorkshire AND North West England
Founder: Joe Buckley
Established: 2017
Threat level: HIGH — closest brand-name and positioning collision

#### What they're doing well

- Repair-first language that closely mirrors Heritage Joiners' philosophy: framing around restoring originals "rather than ending up in the skip" and helping windows "last another 100 years."
- English Heritage collaboration — works with local councils, conservation officers, and English Heritage on listed buildings and in conservation areas. Powerful trust signal.
- Guild of Master Craftsmen membership.
- Broad service stack: sash, box sash, timber windows, stained glass, metal windows, restoration, doors, commercial, trade supply, spray painting.
- Commercial + residential split — equipped for bigger contracts.
- Trade supply arm — builds B2B relationships across the North West and Yorkshire.
- Two decades of team experience as a headline claim.

#### Weaknesses / gaps you can exploit

- Manchester-based, not local to West Yorkshire — coverage is online and via travel. They are not on the ground in WF postcodes.
- Replacement is also offered — "replication of wooden windows and doors" alongside restoration. Repair-first language is slightly undermined by replacement availability.
- Ltd company structure with a team — less personal than a single named craftsman with 35+ years' direct experience.
- No published pricing — same as Heritage Joiners' position, but means there's no defensive moat.
- Brand-name collision — "Heritage Joinery Solutions Ltd" and "Heritage Joiners" sit very close in the search space. Direct risk for Yorkshire searches.
- Founded 2017 — shorter trading history than Rob's 35+ years individually.

#### Heritage Joiners opportunities

1. Outflank them on local presence
   - GBP claimed in Pontefract, Wakefield, Castleford. Local citations, local case studies, local credentials. They cannot match this from Manchester.
2. Out-personal them
   - Founder story, single craftsman, 35+ years, apprenticeship background, listed buildings track record. Ltd companies cannot replicate the personal-trust signal.
3. Tighter repair-first stance
   - HJSL offers replacement. Heritage Joiners' position is sharper: replacement only where repair is genuinely not viable.
4. Brand confusion management
   - Monitor SERP overlap for "heritage joiner Yorkshire," "heritage joiners Pontefract/Wakefield/Leeds."
- GBP, schema, and meta titles must hammer the location identifiers (Pontefract, WF8) to differentiate.
   - If confusion materially affects enquiries, the contingency in the business plan applies: revisit naming with a location or founder anchor.
5. Pursue equivalent credentialling
   - English Heritage collaboration is strong proof. Heritage Joiners' Wakefield Council conservation approval pursuit is the right local equivalent.

#### Evidence captured

- Headline: "Expert Manchester Joiners"
- Repair-language: "restoring them to their original beauty rather than ending up in the skip"; windows over 100 years old, helping them last another 100
- Services: sash, box sash, timber, stained glass, metal, restoration, doors, commercial, trade, spray painting
- Trust signals: Guild of Master Craftsmen, English Heritage collaboration, two decades of team experience
- Has a Locations page covering Yorkshire from Manchester
