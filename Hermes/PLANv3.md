## 1) Mission lock (final)

Build Heritage Joiners into Yorkshire’s leading specialist for listed/period timber repair using a repair-first model, without pretending everything is repairable.

### Fixed positioning language (use exactly)
**fully qualified, time served, bench hand joiner and site carpenter**

Use this phrase consistently across homepage/about/core trust blocks.

### Strategic rule
- Repair-first by default.
- Not repair-at-any-cost.
- If timber retention is too low for a sound repair, recommend selective/full replacement.

## 6) Content framework (mandatory for every service page)

Target length: **700–900 words** per service page.

Required sections:
1. H1 with primary keyword and natural local cue.
2. Repair-first opener.
3. What we repair (8–12 scoped bullets).
4. When repair is viable.
5. When replacement is recommended.
6. Typical cost bands (hedged language).
7. Craft quality block:
   - colour matching
   - distressing to blend-in
   - stains/oils/waxes finishing
8. Listed building practicality notes.
9. FAQ section (4–5 questions).
10. Internal links to related services/areas/case studies.
11. CTA block (use placeholder contact values as-is).

### Pricing bands (use consistently)
- Site assessment: £80–£150
- Small localised repairs: £180–£350
- Standard repair jobs: £350–£900
- Complex repairs: £900+
- Full replacement comparator (sash equivalent): £2,500–£4,000

For structural pages (beam/joist/rafter/frame): emphasize survey-led variability and avoid fixed promises.
